10835 Raven Lake, West Shore Township

Algonquin Highlands (Sherborne), Ontario P0A 1E0

Our private waterfront escape on Raven Lake offers breathtaking Canadian Shield landscapes, surrounded by granite, pine forests, and abundant wildlife. Enjoy direct access to the water, a screened room, private dock, workshop, and endless opportunities for swimming, paddling, and boating. A peaceful cottage retreat where nature, comfort, and true lake life come together.
